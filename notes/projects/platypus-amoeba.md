# platypus-amoeba

## Overview

`platypus-amoeba` is an offline interactive virtual pet / AI familiar housed in a soft, squishy silicone enclosure. It incorporates an internal flexible LED matrix for visual feedback and uses on-device sensory classification (TinyML) and dynamic state logic to detect physical interaction (petting, squishing, handling) and express shifting preferences for how it wants to be touched.

---

## 1. Physical Enclosure & Internal Hardware

- **Enclosure**: Molded soft silicone elastomer (translucent/diffuse, pliable).
- **Display**: Flexible LED matrix mounted internally, illuminating and diffusing through the silicone walls.
- **Microcontroller / Compute**: Adafruit Metro ESP32-S3 (running fully offline with no internet dependency).
  - 240 MHz dual-core Xtensa LX7 with vector acceleration for TinyML.
  - 512 KB SRAM + 8 MB PSRAM.
  - Native hardware capacitive touch peripheral (`TOUCH1`–`TOUCH14`).
- **Feedback & Actuation**:
  - Visual: Diffuse animated facial expressions, mood gradients, and pulse patterns via the flexible LED matrix.
  - Acoustic: Piezo buzzer (PWM) or I2S mini-speaker for purrs, chirp frequencies, or dissonance tones.
  - Haptic: Internal vibration motor / ERM for tactile heartbeat or purr feedback.

## 2. Interaction Sensing & Transduction

### A. Capacitive Touch & Stroke Classification (Surface Petting)
- **Previous Test**: Bare wires inside silicone resulted in erratic readings due to parasitic coupling, surface leakage, and dielectric variability.
- **Engineering Solution**:
  - Electrodes must be **insulated** (copper tape or conductive fabric sealed with Kapton tape or embedded within the silicone wall).
  - Touch sensing operates capacitively through the silicone's dielectric layer ($\kappa \approx 2.7–3.2$) without direct electrical contact.
  - Multi-pad array along the top surface connects to ESP32-S3 touch pins or an MPR121 capacitive controller to track stroke direction, contact area, and duration.

### B. Internal Pneumatic / Barometric Sensing (Squish & Squeeze)
- A digital barometric pressure sensor (e.g., BMP280 or LPS22HB) positioned inside a sealed or semi-sealed hollow silicone cavity.
- Measures instantaneous air pressure spikes resulting from physical compression or squeezing of the body.
- Operates independently of surface conductivity, contact resistance, or ambient illumination.

### C. Active Optical Sensing (Internal Scatter)
- **Previous Test**: Phototransistor array required an overhead ambient light source to register shadows, making baseline thresholds erratic.
- **Engineering Solution**:
  - **Self-Illuminated IR**: Internal reflective infrared sensors (e.g., TCRT5000 or VCNL4040) emitting and measuring backscatter through the silicone.
  - **LED Matrix Backscatter**: Reading internal light reflections with phototransistors situated between matrix pixels as the silicone walls deform.

### D. Inertial / Mechanical Wave Sensing (IMU)
- A 6-DOF IMU (e.g., LIS3DH or LSM6DSOX) mounted to the rigid internal electronics core.
- Samples micro-vibrations and surface acoustic waves generated by stroking, tapping, picking up, or rocking the enclosure.

## 3. On-Device AI Architecture (Offline TinyML)

Running without cloud connectivity, the Metro ESP32-S3 handles real-time inference via an Edge Impulse / TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers (TFLM) pipeline:

1. **Input Features**: Rolling time-series window (1.0–1.5 seconds) of multi-sensor data (capacitive touch channels, internal pressure delta, accelerometer vibrations).
2. **Classifier Model**: Lightweight 1D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) (~15–30 KB flash footprint).
3. **Classified Stroke Categories**:
   - `slow_gentle_stroke`
   - `rapid_patting`
   - `deep_squish`
   - `rough_rub`
   - `idle_untouched`

---

## 4. Changeable Preference & Behavioral Engine

The AI classifier outputs the detected interaction style, which is evaluated against an internal dynamic preference state machine:

```
[ Sensor Stream ] ──> [ TinyML Classifier ] ──> Detected: "rapid_patting"
                                                        │
                                                        ▼
[ Internal State Engine ] ──────────────────> [ Preference Match Evaluator ]
  • current_preference = "slow_gentle_stroke"          │
  • satisfaction_score                                  ├── Match: Affection / Purr
  • boredom_timer                                       └── Mismatch: Irritation / Growl
  • habituation_threshold
```

### Preference Shift Mechanics
- **Satiation / Boredom**: Sustained input of the currently favored pet style raises satisfaction to a threshold, prompting the state machine to transition to a new preference (e.g., from `slow_gentle_stroke` to `deep_squish`).
- **Over-stimulation / Fatigue**: Excessive continuous interaction flips the desired state to `idle_untouched` (requiring a rest period).
- **Habituation & Drift**: Stochastic drift timers periodically alter the baseline preference during quiet periods.
