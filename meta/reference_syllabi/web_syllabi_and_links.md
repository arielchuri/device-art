# External Device Art Syllabi & Research Portals

## 1. Parsons BFA DT Device Art Syllabus (Wordpress Archive)
- **URL**: [https://bfadtdeviceart.wordpress.com/syllabus/](https://bfadtdeviceart.wordpress.com/syllabus/)
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Weekly breakdowns of Japanese media art, Maywa Denki, physical interaction dynamics, and mechanical play.
  - Project progressions: Rapid cardboard prototyping $\to$ Physical transducer hacking $\to$ Custom interactive device exhibition.

## 2. University of Tsukuba VR Lab — Device Art Project Portal
- **URL**: [https://sites.google.com/a/vrlab.esys.tsukuba.ac.jp/da/home-1?authuser=0](https://sites.google.com/a/vrlab.esys.tsukuba.ac.jp/da/home-1?authuser=0)
- **Principal Researchers**: Prof. Hiroo Iwata, Machiko Kusahara.
- **Key Themes**:
  - Integration of advanced mechatronics, haptics, and display technology into artistic form.
  - Commercialization and exhibition of device art in galleries vs. consumer markets.
