# HUMANS.md — Instructor & Contributor Field Guide for `meta/`

Welcome! This directory is the **instructor backstage** for designing, revising, and running **Device Art**.

---

## What Lives in Each Subfolder:

- **[`meta/course-description.md`](course-description.md)**: The official New School course catalog description and studio premise.
- **[`meta/learning-outcomes.md`](learning-outcomes.md)**: The 5 core Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) spanning concept, circuitry, code, fabrication, and documentation.
- **[`meta/readings_source_pool/`](readings_source_pool/)**: PDF readings, Chindōgu philosophies, and bibliography organized by topic.
- **[`meta/reference_syllabi/`](reference_syllabi/)**: Prior Parsons syllabi (Churi, Bidegain, McClendon) and external course links (Tsukuba VR Lab, Wordpress archive).
- **[`meta/pedagogy_and_notes/`](pedagogy_and_notes/)**: Hands-on lab materials imported from *Emergent Objects* (CircuitPython, sensor pinouts, cardboard engineering, hardware kit specs).
- **[`meta/teaching_routines/`](teaching_routines/)**: Operational workflows, weekly pacing checklists, and grading routines.
