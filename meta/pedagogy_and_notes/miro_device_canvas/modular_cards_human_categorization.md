# Proposed Human-Centric Categorization for `modular_cards`

Instead of technical engineering buckets (Inputs, Sensors, Outputs, MCU), this organization aligns cards around **human experience, interaction affordances, physical craft, and environmental perception**.

---

## 1. `01_human_gestures_actions` (What the Human Does)
*Tangible human physical inputs, verbs, and interactions.*
- **Physical Controls**: `01_knob_smooth.svg`, `02_knob_spring_push.svg`, `03_push_button.svg`, `04_slider.svg`, `05_toggle_switch.svg`, `06_potentiometer_rotary.svg`, `07_potentiometer_slider.svg`, `08_potentiometer_trim.svg`
- **Surface Touch & Gestures**: `03_touch_button.svg`, `05_touch_screen.svg`, `05_touchscreen_gesture.svg`, `06_touch_pad.svg`
- **Interaction Verbs**: `verb_01_turn_cw.svg`, `verb_02_turn_ccw.svg`, `verb_03_press_momentary.svg`, `verb_04_press_and_hold.svg`, `verb_05_release.svg`, `verb_06_double_tap.svg`, `verb_07_drag_slide.svg`
- **Body & Physical Rituals**: `action_01_user_human.svg`, `action_02_press_tap.svg`, `action_03_press_hold.svg`, `action_04_turn_rotate.svg`, `action_05_swipe_gesture.svg`, `action_12_speak_voice.svg`, `action_13_shake_agitate.svg`, `action_14_wear_body.svg`, `stage_01_flip_invert.svg`, `stage_02_fold_unfold.svg`, `stage_03_open_close_lid.svg`, `stage_04_squeeze_compress.svg`, `stage_05_blow_breath.svg`, `stage_06_insert_slot.svg`, `stage_07_dock_cradle.svg`, `stage_08_rub_stroke.svg`
