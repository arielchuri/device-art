# First Class "Hook" & Excitement Strategy

## Goal for Night 1 (Week 01)
Get students thrilled about what they will create by bypassing dry administrative talk and immediately demonstrating the magical intersection of **physical form, whimsical function, and expressive electronics**.

---

## 3 Demonstrations to Show Night 1

### 1. The Interactive "Device Art" Live Showcase
- **Concept**: Bring 2–3 completed, highly polished physical artifacts from previous semesters or personal work (e.g. *The Buddha Box audio player*, *The Cardboard Shadow Projector*, or a *Custom NeoPixel / Mechanical Shutter*).
- **In-Class Action**: Pass the physical objects around. Let students touch the switches, adjust dials, hear the audio transducers, and see custom laser-cut/cast enclosures in real life.
- **Message**: "By Week 15, you will design, engineer, and fabricate your own standalone interactive device."

### 2. Live Rapid Breadboard Demo (5-Minute Miracle)
- Plug a Raspberry Pi Pico into a laptop.
- Wire a potentiometer to an analog pin and an OLED screen or NeoPixel ring in 2 minutes.
- Live-code 10 lines of CircuitPython showing instantaneous physical response to human touch.
- Demystify the tech: "Electronics are not an intimidating black box—they are a medium like clay, wood, or paint."

### 3. Deconstructing Commercial Black Boxes (The Device Art Manifesto)
- Show contrasting examples of sterile consumer devices (smartphones, voice assistants) vs. **Device Art** (devices that critique, entertain, delight, or challenge societal norms).
- Introduce the *Anti-Virtual* design perspective: physical knobs, textures, haptics, and humor over flat glass screens.
