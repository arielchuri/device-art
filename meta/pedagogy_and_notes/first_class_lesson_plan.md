## 2. Timed Class Outline & Run-of-Show

### 19:00 – 19:15 (15 min) | Arrival, Warm-up & Attendance
- Welcome students as they walk in.
- Have a slide / whiteboard note up: Course name, your name, and a prompt for intros.
- Mark attendance on Canvas Roll Call.

### 19:15 – 19:45 (30 min) | The "Hook": What is Device Art?
- **Pass Around Physical Artifacts**: Hand working physical devices to the students. Let them flip switches, turn knobs, and see real enclosures.
- **The Core Premise**:
  - What is Device Art? (The mechanism IS the art; breaking out of flat glass screens).
  - Contrast sterile consumer gadgets (smart speakers, phones) with expressive/provocative devices.
  - Introduce the dual traditions: **Chindōgu & Play** (Kenji Kawakami, Maywa Denki) and **Material Resistance & Subversion** (V&A Disobedient Objects).

### 19:45 – 20:15 (30 min) | Student & Instructor Introductions
- Brief intro of your background (Sparkle Labs, design, physical computing).
- Student round-robin (Keep to ~1–2 min each):
  1. Name & Major (Design & Tech, Fine Arts, Product Design, etc.).
  2. Experience level with electronics or code (reassure beginners that no prior coding/circuit experience is required).
  3. One everyday physical object or tool they love, hate, or find strange.

### 20:15 – 20:25 (10 min) | Bio Break / Mid-Class Pause

### 20:25 – 20:50 (25 min) | The 5-Minute Live Breadboard Demo
- Plug a Raspberry Pi Pico into your laptop on the projector.
- Show how 10 lines of CircuitPython make an LED blink, read a potentiometer dial, or print to an OLED screen.
- Demystify the tech: "Electronics are just raw materials like cardboard, acrylic, or clay."

### 20:50 – 21:20 (30 min) | Syllabus, Tools & Hardware Kits Walkthrough
- **Review Course Structure**:
  - Show Canvas site and syllabus overview.
  - Explain grading (1/3 Projects, 1/3 Labs, 1/3 Participation).
- **Explain the Hardware Kit ($44.50)**:
  - Show the physical parts list.
  - Explain that kits will be handed out in **Week 2**.
  - Show payment options (Venmo/Zelle/PayPal/Cash) and clarify self-sourcing option on Canvas.

### 21:20 – 21:40 (20 min) | Week 1 Homework & Wrap-Up
- **Assignment for Week 2**:
  1. Download & install **Thonny** or **Mu Editor** / VS Code on their laptops.
  2. Read Machiko Kusahara's *Device Art* essay and *The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu* on Canvas.
  3. Post 3 friction/absurdity ideas to the Canvas discussion board.
- Open Q&A and wrap up by 9:40 PM.
