# Bernie Lubell — Mechanical Wood & Analog Computing

- **Artist**: Bernie Lubell
- **Official Site**: [https://bernielubell.com/](https://bernielubell.com/)
- **Themes**: Wood Joinery, Hand-Cranked Mechanisms, Pneumatic Memory, Acoustic Computation.

---

## Notable Works

1. **Aether (1999)**
   * *Description*: An elaborate interactive mechanical apparatus built entirely of wood, latex, and string that models physical fluid dynamics and human breath.
2. **Sufficient Explanations (2010)**
   * *Description*: Interactive wooden mechanical installations where user cranking drives mechanical cams, air pumps, and bellows, making visible the physical logic of computation without electronics.
