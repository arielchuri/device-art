# Dunne & Raby — Critical Design & Design Noir

- **Designers**: Anthony Dunne & Fiona Raby
- **Official Site**: [http://www.dunneandraby.co.uk/](http://www.dunneandraby.co.uk/)
- **Key Texts**: *Design Noir: The Secret Life of Electronic Objects*, *Speculative Everything*
- **Themes**: Electronic Furniture, Electromagnetic Landscapes, Value Friction, Speculative Hardware.

---

## Notable Works & Projects

1. **Faraday Chair (1997)**
   * *Description*: A low-tech transparent domestic shelter and acrylic cocoon lined with copper mesh, shielding the inhabitant from all electromagnetic radiation.

2. **Placebo Project (2001)**
   * *Description*: Eight domestic electronic objects designed to elicit attitudes toward electromagnetic radiation (e.g. *Compass Table*, which spins erratic dials when a laptop or cell phone is set upon it).

3. **Hertzian Tales Projects (1999)**
   * *Description*: Radios and transceivers tuned to reveal the invisible ethereal traffic of urban radio spectrums.
