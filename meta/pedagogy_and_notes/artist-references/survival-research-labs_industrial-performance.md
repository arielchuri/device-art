# Survival Research Laboratories (SRL) — Industrial Machine Performance

- **Founder**: Mark Pauline
- **Origin**: San Francisco, CA (1978–present)
- **Official Site**: [https://srl.org/](https://srl.org/)
- **Themes**: Kinetic Machinery, Reclaimed Industrial Hardware, Tele-operation, Anti-Consumerist Spectacle.

---

## Key Technological & Artistic Elements

1. **The V1 Flying Bomb Engine / Pitching Machine**
   * *Description*: Extreme kinetic apparatuses constructed from surplus military hardware and heavy industrial pneumatic actuators.
2. **Tele-Robotic Combatants**
   * *Description*: Pneumatic and hydraulic walking machines controlled via remote microwave radio links, subverting sterile showroom robotics into visceral mechanical theater.
