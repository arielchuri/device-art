# Kelly Heaton — Electronic Animism & Analog Circuit Sculptures

- **Artist**: Kelly Heaton
- **Official Site**: [https://kellyheatonstudio.com/](https://kellyheatonstudio.com/)
- **Themes**: Transistor Level Acoustics, Non-Microcontroller Electronics, Nature Simulation, Circuit Topography.

---

## Notable Works

1. **The Circuit Birds (2018–present)**
   * *Description*: Unprogrammed analog circuits designed on raw copper clad boards where discrete transistors, capacitors, and resistors generate naturalistic bird songs without any microprocessors or software.
2. **The Reflection Machine (2001)**
   * *Description*: 400 disassembled Furby robotic eyes and motors wired to an interactive mirror camera that tracks human gaze.
