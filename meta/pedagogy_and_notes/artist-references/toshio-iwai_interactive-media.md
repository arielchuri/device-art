# Toshio Iwai (岩井俊雄) — Tangible Audio-Visual Instruments

- **Artist**: Toshio Iwai
- **Origin**: Japan
- **Themes**: Interactive Screen-Object Hybrids, Visual Music, Physical Musicality.

---

## Notable Works & Projects

1. **Tenori-On (2007, with Yamaha)**
   * *Description*: A magnesium alloy handheld 16x16 LED matrix musical instrument where light and sound are physically synchronized.
   * *Significance*: A hallmark of the **Device Art** movement—transitioning from gallery installation to a mass-manufactured musical instrument.

2. **Music on the Surface (1993)**
   * *Description*: Physical tracks and mechanical sliders projected with dynamic real-time graphics and MIDI synthesis.

3. **Electroplankton (Nintendo DS, 2005)**
   * *Description*: Interactive audio-visual software treating the handheld gaming device as a tactile synthesizer.
