# Victoria & Albert Museum — Disobedient Objects

- **Curators**: Catherine Flood & Gavin Grindon
- **Exhibition Archive**: [https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/disobedient-objects](https://www.vam.ac.uk/exhibitions/disobedient-objects)
- **Themes**: Tactical Material Culture, Guerrilla Hardware, Grassroots Fabrication, Direct Action.

---

## Notable Objects in the Collection

1. **BookBloc Shields (Italy/UK, 2010–2011)**
   * *Description*: High-density foam riot shields painted as classic philosophy and literature covers (e.g. Orwell, Benjamin, Boccaccio) used in student protests.

2. **Guerrilla Mobile Projector / Bike Beamer (Spain/US)**
   * *Description*: Battery-powered digital projectors mounted to bicycle handlebars and mobile chassis to project political slogans on building facades.

3. **Phone Jammers & Radio Silence Pouches**
   * *Description*: Low-cost Faraday pouches and portable physical shields to prevent cell tower tracking during assemblies.

4. **Tear Gas Mask from Plastic Bottles**
   * *Description*: A 2-liter soda bottle sliced and fitted with activated charcoal aquarium filters for emergency protection.
