# Steve Mann — Wearable Computing & Sousveillance

- **Researcher/Artist**: Steve Mann (MIT Media Lab / University of Toronto)
- **Official Site**: [http://wearcam.org/](http://wearcam.org/)
- **Themes**: Sousveillance (Inverse Surveillance), Wearable Cyborg Interfaces, Humanistic Intelligence.

---

## Key Contributions & Hardware

1. **EyeTap & Wearable Displays (1970s–present)**
   * *Description*: Custom laser/prism eyeglass frames that project rays of computer-generated light directly onto the retina while recording the user's viewpoint.
2. **The Concept of "Sousveillance" (Watching from Below)**
   * *Description*: Using personal, wearable recording and sensing devices to monitor authorities and corporate surveillance infrastructure.
