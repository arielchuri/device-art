# Natalie Jeremijenko — Environmental Media & Tactical Devices

- **Artist/Engineer**: Natalie Jeremijenko
- **Affiliation**: Environmental Health Clinic / NYU
- **Official Site / Portfolio**: [http://www.environmentalhealthclinic.net/](http://www.environmentalhealthclinic.net/)
- **Themes**: Tangible Interfaces, Participatory Science, Biomechanical Prototyping, Tactical Media.

---

## Notable Works & Projects

1. **Live Wire / Dangling String (1995)** (at Xerox PARC with Mark Weiser)
   * *Description*: An 8-foot piece of plastic string connected to a small electric stepper motor hanging from the ceiling. It physically twitched and whirred in proportion to local Ethernet network traffic.
   * *Significance*: The foundational masterpiece of **Ambient Interaction & Calm Technology**.

2. **Feral Robotic Dogs (2002–2006)**
   * *Description*: Low-cost commercial robotic toy dogs modified with custom gas sensors and microcontroller logic, released by communities into toxic brownfields to sniff out environmental contaminants.

3. **OOZ (2003–present)**
   * *Description*: Interactive technology installed in urban habitats enabling human-animal communication (interactive robotic geese, bird perches with visual translation).
