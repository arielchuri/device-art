# Kenji Kawakami (川上賢司) — Chindōgu & Unuseless Inventions

- **Creator**: Kenji Kawakami
- **Origin**: Japan (1980s–present)
- **Organization**: International Chindōgu Society (ICS)
- **Key Reference**: *101 Unuseless Japanese Inventions* / *99 More Unuseless Japanese Inventions*
- **Themes**: Physical Absurdism, The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu, Anti-Commercialization, Everyday Problem-Solving.

---

## Notable Inventions

1. **Butter Stick (バタースティック)**
   * *Description*: A glue-stick twist tube loaded with butter for rapid toast spread.
2. **Eye Drop Funnel Glasses (点眼眼鏡)**
   * *Description*: Eyeglasses equipped with miniature funnels over each eye to ensure eye drops never miss.
3. **Hayfever Toilet Paper Hat (花粉症トイレットペーパーハット)**
   * *Description*: A mounted roll of toilet paper worn atop the head for continuous napkin access during allergy season.
4. **Baby Floor-Mop Onesie (赤ちゃんモップ)**
   * *Description*: Infant clothing stitched with microfiber cleaning pads to dust hardwood floors while crawling.
