# Maywa Denki (明和電機) — Nonsense Machines & Commercial Artifacts

- **Artist/Group**: Novmichi Tosa / Maywa Denki
- **Origin**: Japan (1993–present)
- **Official Website**: [https://www.maywadenki.com/](https://www.maywadenki.com/)
- **Themes**: Whimsical Mechatronics, Corporate Parody, Parallel Utility, Acoustic Transducers, Mass Product Commercialization.

---

## Notable Works & Projects

1. **Otamatone (オタマトーン)**
   * *Description*: Electronic musical instrument synthesizer shaped like an eighth note with a singing mouth, operated via ribbon touch-strip and squeeze expression.
   * *Significance*: One of the most commercially successful Device Art creations worldwide.

2. **Naki Series (Fish-Motif Inventions)**
   * *Description*: 26 absurd devices exploring mortality, electricity, and nonsense utility built around fish skeletons and aquatic forms.

3. **Edelweiss Series**
   * *Description*: Feminine automata and mechanized flower objects exploring porcelain aesthetics, music boxes, and mechanical computation.

4. **Tsukuba Series (Physical Sound Generators)**
   * *Description*: Electric-strike percussion units, solenoid clackers (*Pachi-Moku* finger-snap trigger backpack), and automated acoustic instruments.
