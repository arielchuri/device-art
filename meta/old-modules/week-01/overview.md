# Week 01: Introductions, Course Philosophy & What is Device Art?

## Objectives
- Introduce the conceptual framework of **Device Art** (breaking out of flat glass screens into physical mechanisms).
- Explore the dual traditions of speculative hardware: **Chindōgu & Play** (Kenji Kawakami, Maywa Denki) and **Material Resistance & Subversion** (Disobedient Objects).
- Review course structure, grading policy, and required hardware prototyping kits ($50.00).
- Introduce the development environment (Thonny, VS Code, Python).

## In-Class Activities & Agenda
1. **The Hook & Inspiration Showcase**: Pass around physical artifacts (custom enclosures, switches, rotary dials).
2. **Student & Instructor Introductions**: Round-robin on physical objects students love, hate, or find strange.
3. **5-Minute Live Breadboard Demo**: Plugging in a Raspberry Pi Pico and lighting an LED in 10 lines of Python.
4. **Syllabus & Kit Walkthrough**: Reviewing materials, payment options, and the schedule.

## Readings & Media
- Machiko Kusahara, *Device Art: A New Form of Media Art from a Japanese Perspective* (2006)
- Kenji Kawakami, *The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu*
- [Sparkle Labs Discover Electronics Overview](https://vimeo.com/channels/sparlelabs)
