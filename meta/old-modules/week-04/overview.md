# Week 04: Prototyping & Sensor Labs (Analog Inputs, PWM & Actuation)

## Objectives
- Master analog-to-digital conversion (ADC) on the RP2040 (reading raw 16-bit values `0–65535`).
- Calibrate and map analog sensors (photoresistors / LDRs, potentiometers) to meaningful physical units.
- Generate Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM) signals for variable LED dimming and speaker pitch synthesis.
- Introduce digital sensing: capacitive touch interfaces (TTP223) and ultrasonic range finding (HC-SR04).
- Brainstorm and scope project proposals for **Project 1: Speculative Device Prototype**.

## In-Class Activities & Agenda
1. **Sensor Calibration Bench**: Probing analog photocells and potentiometers with `analogio`.
2. **PWM Dimming & Tone Lab**: Mapping light levels to LED brightness curves and audio tones on the 8-ohm speaker.
3. **Capacitive Touch Workshop**: Creating custom foil and conductive touchpads.
4. **Project 1 Ideation & Miro Review**: Reviewing speculative friction ideas and enclosure plans.
