# Readings Source Pool & Course Bibliography

This catalog describes all reference texts, readings, and papers organized by category for instructor selection and module assignment.

---

## 1. Theory, Philosophy & Device Art (`theory_and_art/`)

* **Machiko Kusahara — *Device Art: A New Approach in Understanding Japanese Media Art***
  * *Focus*: Foundational definition of Device Art. Explores how technology, craft, playfulness, and commercial product form merge into art.
* **Anthony Dunne & Fiona Raby — *Design Noir: The Secret Life of Electronic Objects***
  * *Focus*: Critical design, electronic objects that explore psychological dimensions of everyday life rather than pure utility.
* **Brenda Laurel — *Computers as Theatre***
  * *Focus*: Interaction as dramatic performance and narrative experience.
* **Bill Moggridge — *Designing Interactions***
  * *Focus*: Historical case studies of prototype iterations and interface design philosophies.

---

## 2. Electronics & Physical Computing (`electronics/`)

* **Ariel Churi & Sparkle Labs — *Electricity Fundamentals & Breadboard Guides***
  * *Focus*: Direct voltage/current/resistance heuristics, multimeter debugging, sensor signal conditioning.
* **Tom Igoe & Dan O'Sullivan — *Physical Computing: Sensing and Controlling the Physical World with Computers***
  * *Focus*: Transducers, analog vs. digital inputs, actuator dynamics, and microcontroller architectures.
* **Charles Platt — *Make: Electronics (Component Experiments)***
  * *Focus*: Hands-on experiential learning through component testing and circuit assembly.

---

## 3. Prototyping & Fabrication (`prototyping/`)

* **Cardboard Engineering & Rapid Physical Modeling Guide**
  * *Focus*: Structural cardboard techniques, folded joints, internal mounting chassis, and rapid mockups.
* **Digital Fabrication Primer (Laser Cutting, 3D Printing, Casting & Molding)**
  * *Focus*: Tolerances, snap-fits, living hinges, silicone casting, and material properties.
* **Product Photography & Visual Documentation Handbook**
  * *Focus*: Documenting physical work with proper studio lighting, video demonstration, and exploded-view diagrams.

---

## 4. Code & Embedded Systems (`code/`)

* **CircuitPython & MicroPython for Microcontrollers**
  * *Focus*: State machines, event loops, non-blocking timing, I2C/SPI sensor interfacing.
* **Python Programming Fundamentals for Artists & Designers**
  * *Focus*: Logic, data structures, serial communication, and modular code architecture.
