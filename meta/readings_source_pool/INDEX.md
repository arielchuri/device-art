# Readings Source Pool & Course Bibliography

This catalog describes all reference texts, readings, and papers organized by category for instructor selection and module assignment.

---

## 1. Theory, Philosophy & Device Art (`theory_and_art/`)

* **Machiko Kusahara — *Device Art: A New Form of Media Art from a Japanese Perspective* (2006)** (`2006_Kusahara_Device_Art_A_New_Form.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Foundational manifesto defining Device Art. Examines the convergence of art, technology, playfulness, and commercial product form in Japanese culture.
* **Kenji Kawakami — *The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu (The Art of the Unuseless)*** (`chindogu_and_device_art.md`)
  * *Focus*: Philosophical guidelines for creating physically functional yet practically absurd inventions.
* **Anthony Dunne & Fiona Raby — *Speculative Everything: Design, Fiction, and Social Dreaming*** (`speculativeEverything.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Critical design, electronic objects that explore conceptual, cultural, and psychological dimensions.
* **Johan Huizinga — *Homo Ludens: A Study of the Play-Element in Culture*** (`homoludens.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: The centrality of play, games, and whimsical instinct in human civilization and art creation.
* **Catherine Flood & Gavin Grindon — *Disobedient Objects (V&A Exhibition)*** (`DisobedientObjects.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Material culture of resistance; how everyday physical objects and tools are modified and repurposed for direct action.
* **Alastair Fuad-Luke — *Design Activism: Beautiful Strangeness for a Sustainable World*** (`designactivism-beautifulstrangenessforasustainableworld_alastairfuadluke.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Counter-narratives in product design, anti-consumerist objects, and transformative design methods.
* **Harry Brignull — *Dark Patterns: Deceptive Design in Tech*** (`darkpatterns.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Analyzing coercive interaction design and how physical device art can subvert commercial digital traps.
* **John Zerzan — *Running on Emptiness: The Failure of Symbolic Thought*** (`John_Zerzan__Running_on_Emptiness__The_Failure_of_Symbolic_Thought_a4.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Radical critique of technology, alienation, and symbolic mediation.
* **Kelli Anderson — *Curious Things for Curious People*** (`Curious_things_for_curious_people.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Tactile paper engineering, functional analog devices, and tangible interaction wonders.

## 2. Electronics & Physical Computing (`electronics/`)

* **Charles Platt — *Make: Electronics (Learning by Discovery)*** (`make-electronics.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Experiential electronics benchwork, hands-on component experiments, and multimeter testing.
* **Forrest M. Mims III — *Getting Started in Electronics*** (`mims-gettingstarted-basics.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Iconic hand-drawn circuit schematics, semiconductor basics, and fundamental analog behavior.
* **Tom Igoe & Dan O'Sullivan — *Physical Computing: Sensing and Controlling the Physical World*** (`osullivan-igoe-sensors.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Transducers, analog vs. digital sensor inputs, signal conditioning, and microcontroller integration.
* **Paul Scherz & Simon Monk — *Practical Electronics for Inventors: Switches & Relays*** (`scherz-switches.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Mechanical switch mechanisms, debouncing, relay isolation, and electrical power control.
* **Massimo Banzi & Michael Shiloh — *Getting Started with Arduino*** (`Getting_Started_with_Arduino.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Open-source physical computing philosophy, rapid prototyping, and interactive design loops.

---

## 3. Prototyping & Fabrication Methods (`prototyping/`)

* **Stephanie Houde & Charles Hill — *What Do Prototypes Prototype?*** (`houde-prototypes.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Foundational interaction design model distinguishing prototypes of *Role*, *Look and Feel*, and *Implementation*.
* **Marc Stickdorn et al. — *This Is Service Design Doing: Prototyping Methods (Ch 07)*** (`prototyping_tisdd_method_ch07.pdf` & `TISDD_methods.pdf`)
  * *Focus*: Staging physical interactions, desktop walkthroughs, cardboard mockups, and iterative user testing.
