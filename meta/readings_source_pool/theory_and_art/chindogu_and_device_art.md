# Chindōgu (珍道具) & Device Art: The Art of the Unuseless

## 1. What is Chindōgu?
Coined by Japanese inventor and writer **Kenji Kawakami** in the 1990s, *Chindōgu* literally translates to "unusual tool" or "unuseless invention." 

A Chindōgu is an object that appears to solve a specific everyday problem, but in practice creates so many new problems, absurdities, or social embarrassment that it is practically "unuseless."

---

## 2. The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu (Kenji Kawakami)

1. **A Chindōgu cannot be for real use**: It must be practically useless, though physically functional.
2. **A Chindōgu must exist**: You must actually make the physical thing. It cannot just be an idea on paper.
3. **Inherent in every Chindōgu is the spirit of anarchy**: They are free from the shackles of commercial utility and cost-benefit analysis.
4. **Chindōgu are tools for everyday life**: They must address recognizable, universal daily situations.
5. **Chindōgu are not for sale**: If you accept money for a Chindōgu, you surrender its purity.
6. **Humor must not be the sole reason for creating a Chindōgu**: The humor arises organically from the earnest, serious attempt to solve a problem with absurd engineering.
7. **Chindōgu is not propaganda**: It is an innocent exploration of physical interaction, not a political billboard.
8. **Chindōgu are never taboo**: No vulgarity or cheap cruelty.
9. **Chindōgu cannot be patented**: They are open gifts to the cultural commons.
10. **Chindōgu are without prejudice**: Everyone is equally capable of enjoying and creating them.

## 3. Intersection with Device Art & Student Projects
- **Maywa Denki (明和電機)**: Bridging Chindōgu, mechanical engineering, nonsense machines (*Otamatone*, *Edelweiss series*), and commercial mass production.
- **Desistive / Adversarial Objects**: Devices that playfully resist compliance or expose the absurdity of smart gadgets.
- **Studio Exercise**: *The 2-Hour Chindōgu Rapid Cardboard / Breadboard Challenge*.
