# Week 02: Electricity Fundamentals, Breadboard Circuits & Multimeter Diagnostics

## Objectives
- Distribute and inventory student physical computing prototyping kits ($50.00).
- Understand atomic charge, voltage (electrical push), current (flow rate), and resistance (impedance).
- Master solderless breadboard internal anatomy, power rails, and terminal strips.
- Use a digital multimeter for continuity beeping, DC voltage verification, and measuring resistor values.
- Calculate LED current-limiting resistors and series/parallel resistor combinations.

## In-Class Activities & Agenda
1. **Kit Distribution & Component Verification**: Unboxing hardware kits and organizing breadboards.
2. **Multimeter Hands-On**: Continuity testing on breadboard rows, jumper wires, and conductors.
3. **Miro Circuit Playground & Bench Prototyping**:
   - Powering an LED driver circuit.
   - Diagnosing open vs. short circuits.
   - Breadboarding series vs. parallel resistor circuits.
