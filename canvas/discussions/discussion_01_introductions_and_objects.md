# Graded Discussion: Hi! My name is _________

- **Points**: 10 Points

## Discussion Prompt

Welcome to Device Art! Use this discussion thread to introduce yourself, share photos of physical objects in your life, and imagine speculative devices across utopian and dystopian futures.

### 1. Photo of Yourself
- Include a clear, well-lit photo of yourself so I can learn your name and connect your face to your work!

---

### 2. Physical Objects in Your World
*(Include clear, well-lit photos or illustrations for each. Remember: **no phones, computers, or tablets**! A physical object of any size—could be a bookmark, favorite pen, LIDAR scanner, specialized tool, kitchen gadget, etc. Tell us what it is and what you like or dislike about it).*

- **An object I use every day**: What is it, and what is its role in your daily routine?
- **An object you like**: What makes this object compelling, elegant, or satisfying to use?
- **An object you dislike**: What friction, frustration, or poor physical interaction does it cause?
- **An object from fiction**: An iconic physical gadget, machine, or tool from a book, movie, comic, or game.

---

### 3. Speculative Devices: Dystopia & Utopia
*(Include a visual reference/image from any source for each).*

- **Dystopian Object**: Imagine the world becomes a terrible place. Imagine a device created for that terrible world. Tell us why the world is a dystopia and what the object does.
- **Utopian Object**: Imagine the world becomes a wonderful place. Imagine a device created for that wonderful world. Tell us why the world is a utopia and what the object does.
