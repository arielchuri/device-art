## Overview

Milestone 1 establishes the conceptual, experiential, and material foundation of your Final Device Art Capstone. In this phase, you define what your device is, how a human encounters and operates it, what artistic precedents inspire it, and what electronic and physical components will bring it to life.

Before writing code or cutting materials, you must clarify your vision, audit your hardware kit, identify components that need to be ordered early, and acknowledge technical unknowns.

Refer to the master [Final Project Overview & Roadmap](../pages/final_project_overview.md) for full context and visual examples.

---

## Required Deliverables

Your Milestone 1 submission consists of three interconnected elements:

### 1. Proposal Document (*Learning Portfolio* Post)
Publish a dedicated post on your Parsons Learning Portfolio containing:
* **Working Title**: An evocative title for your device.
* **Concept Narrative**: What is the object? What cultural, speculative, or personal inquiry does it address? Why does it take the form of an electronic device?
* **User Interaction Narrative**: A step-by-step description of how a human encounters, touches, operates, and experiences the object. Describe the physical affordances (switches, buttons, touch surfaces) and the responsive feedback (light patterns, sound, motion, display text).
* **Artistic & Cultural Precedents**: Links to and short analyses of at least **3 specific projects** (contemporary media art, historical scientific instruments, speculative design, or interactive devices) that inform your direction.
* **Technical Unknowns & Risks**: What aspects of this build are you uncertain about? (e.g., unfamiliar CircuitPython libraries, casting silicone, power consumption, tolerances). What experiments will you run to de-risk them?

### 2. Miro Inspiration Board
Curate a visual research board on [Miro](https://miro.com) and invite `churia@newschool.edu`. Your board must include:
* **At least 20 curated images**: Material finishes, tactile controls, mechanical linkages, industrial design details, lighting effects, and typography.
* **At least 5 case study references**: Annotated with notes on what you are drawing from each piece.
* **Preliminary Form Sketches**: Initial hand-drawn or digital sketches exploring proportions, grip, and control placements.

### 3. Initial Bill of Materials (BOM) & Parts Audit
Create a markdown table or spreadsheet detailing every component you expect to use:
* **Kit Components**: Which parts from your course kit will be used? (Raspberry Pi Pico, OLED SSD1306, LCD1602, Neopixels, Capacitive Touch, Ultrasonic, RTC DS3231, etc.)
* **Sourced Hardware & Raw Materials**: What materials (acrylic, hardwood, silicone, aluminum, readymades) and fasteners (screws, standoff spacers, hinges) will you need to source? Include vendor links and estimated costs.
