## Assignment Overview

To begin our hands-on circuitry and microcontroller labs in Week 02, every student must have their hardware prototyping tools and software environment ready.

### Requirements:

1. **Hardware Kit Verification**:
   - Confirm your hardware kit for Week 02.
   - Either bring your receipt/confirmation for the instructor-provided bundle ($44.50), or bring all independently sourced components to your studio workbench.
   - Required core parts: Raspberry Pi Pico (with headers), Micro-USB data cable & adapter, solderless breadboard, jumper wires, and digital multimeter.

2. **Software Environment Setup**:
   - Install **Thonny** (recommended) or **Mu Editor** / VS Code on your laptop:
     - [Download Thonny IDE](https://thonny.org/)
   - Ensure your laptop can connect to external USB devices.
