# Week 02 Preparation & Core Readings: Electricity & Circuits

Before our hands-on breadboard lab and multimeter diagnostics in studio, review the following essential resources. These readings break down voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's law, and multimeter usage with clear, visual mental models.

---

## 📘 Primary Reading: Discover Electronics Lesson Book
* **File:** [`manual_wm_s.pdf`](/courses/1929836/files/folder/readings?preview=manual_wm_s.pdf) *(Sparkle Labs Discover Electronics Manual)*
* **Focus Sections to Review:**
  * **Pages 4–6: Solderless Breadboard & Atomic Charge** — How internal metal clips connect components, power rails (`+` / `-`), and electron movement.
  * **Page 5: Create a Circuit** — Basic LED driver circuit, polarity (anode vs. cathode), and the role of current-limiting resistors.
  * **Pages 7–8: Volts, Amps, Ohms & Ohm's Law ($V = I \times R$)** — Understanding electrical pressure (Volts), flow rate (Amps/mA), and resistance ($\Omega$).
  * **Pages 9–10: Troubled Circuits** — Diagnosing open circuits vs. short circuits.
  * **Pages 11–12: Light Emitting Diodes & Resistor Sizing** — Forward voltage drop by LED color (Red: 1.7V, Green: 2.6V, Blue: 3.4V) and calculating required protection resistors ($R = \frac{V_S - V_L}{I}$).
  * **Pages 13–14: Resistor Color Codes, Series & Parallel Circuits** — Calculating total resistance for resistors placed in series ($R_{\text{total}} = R_1 + R_2$) and in parallel ($\frac{1}{R_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$).
  * **Pages 15–17: Variable Resistance & Switches** — Photoresistors (LDRs), 10k potentiometers, and tactile pushbuttons.
