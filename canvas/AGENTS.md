# HUMANS.md — Course Content & Canvas Mirror Guide

This directory is the **direct reflection of what goes live to students on Canvas**.

---

## Home Page:
- **`home.md`**: First page the students see with links to important pages and modules.

## Folder Map:
### **`syllabus/`**: The active term syllabus (`syllabus_fall2026.md`) that syncs to the Canvas Syllabus tab.
### **`modules/`**: Organization of course material holding all information being taught to the students which is all of the _Pages_. Files in this folder are named after the names of the module. The content of the files show which pages should be in the modules.
### **`assignments/`**: Assignment briefs with standardized 1-click rubric tables.
### **`pages/`**: The canvas pages that contain the course content.
### **`announcements/`**: Drafted announcements ready to broadcast to students.
### **`discussions/`**: Canvas discussion board prompts.
### **`files/`**: Public student-facing handouts, readings, graphics, and schematics.
### **`grades/`**: Private local grading spreadsheets (100% ignored by Git for student privacy).
