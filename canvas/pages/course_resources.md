# Course Resources & Technical References

Welcome to the central resource hub for **Device Art (PSAM 2230)**. Here you will find documentation, development tools, cheatsheets, fabrication guides, theoretical readings, and component supplier links.

---

## 1. Microcontroller & Physical Computing Documentation

- **Raspberry Pi Pico & Pico W**:
  - [Official Raspberry Pi Pico Documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/microcontrollers/raspberry-pi-pico.html) — Pinout diagrams, specifications, and hardware datasheets.
  - [CircuitPython for Raspberry Pi Pico](https://circuitpython.org/board/raspberry_pi_pico/) — Firmware downloads and flashing instructions.
  - [CircuitPython Libraries Bundle](https://circuitpython.org/libraries) — Standard community driver bundle for sensors, displays, and neopixels.
  - [Adafruit Learning System: Welcome to CircuitPython](https://learn.adafruit.com/welcome-to-circuitpython) — Foundational beginner tutorial for CircuitPython.
- **Circuit Simulators & Learning Portals**:
  - [Wokwi Raspberry Pi Pico Simulator](https://wokwi.com/) — Browser-based virtual Pico, breadboard, and sensor simulator for rapid prototyping without physical hardware.
  - [Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits) — Interactive 3D breadboard and circuit simulation.
  - [Sparkle Labs Discover Electronics Video Tutorials](https://vimeo.com/channels/sparklelabs) — Step-by-step physical computing and circuit demonstrations.

## 2. Software & Development Environments

- **Code Editors**:
  - [Thonny Python IDE](https://thonny.org/) — Recommended beginner-friendly IDE with built-in CircuitPython/MicroPython serial REPL and file manager.
  - [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) — Industry-standard code editor. Recommended extensions: *CircuitPython* by Joedevivo, *Python* by Microsoft.
  - [Mu Editor](https://codewith.mu/) — Simple, lightweight code editor for Python and microcontrollers.
- **Version Control & 3D Fabrication Software**:
  - [GitHub Desktop](https://desktop.github.com/) — Visual interface for Git repositories and code backups.
  - [Blender 3D](https://www.blender.org/) — Open-source 3D modeling, rendering, and speculative object visualization.
  - [Autodesk Fusion 360 (Education Access)](https://www.autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/education-overview) — Parametric CAD for 3D printing and precise mechanical enclosures.

## 4. Theory, Device Art Manifestos & Artist References

Foundational texts and international archives exploring the intersection of art, play, and functional devices:

- **Manifestos & Academic Foundations**:
  - [Machiko Kusahara: Device Art — A New Form of Media Art from a Japanese Perspective (2006)](https://www.isea-symposium-archives.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/2006_Kusahara_Device_Art_A_New_Form.pdf) — Foundational manifesto examining the convergence of technology, playfulness, and commercial product form.
  - [Device Art Project Portal (Tsukuba VR Lab)](https://sites.google.com/a/vrlab.esys.tsukuba.ac.jp/da/home?authuser=0) — International research portal on device art history and interactive installations.
  - [Ars Electronica: Device Art Chronicle](https://ars.electronica.art/outofthebox/en/deviceartchronicle/) & [Cool Tools](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/en/2014/08/12/device-art/) — Historical retrospective and curatorial essays.
  - [Kenji Kawakami: The 10 Tenets of Chindōgu](https://chindogu.com/) — The art of the unuseless and non-utilitarian invention philosophy.
- **Critical & Speculative Design**:
  - [Dunne & Raby: Speculative Everything](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262019910/speculative-everything/) — Design as a catalyst for social dreaming and critical inquiry.
  - [V&A Museum: Disobedient Objects](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/about-disobedient-objects) — Material culture of grass-roots defiance and everyday tool modifications.
  - [Hiroo Iwata: Device Art Feature on Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/technology/hiroo-iwatadevice-art/) — Interactive mechanics and tactile interface design.
  - [George Galanakis: Device Art — The Real Art of Product Design](https://medium.com/hackernoon/the-untitled-document-2-device-art-bdce66e71c36).
