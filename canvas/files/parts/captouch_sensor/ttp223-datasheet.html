Dimensional Guide, Solder Jumper Truth Table, & Enclosure Integration

TOUCH SENSOR MOMENTARY / TOGGLE 2.0V – 5.5V
1. Operational Overview & Specifications

The TTP223 module is a solid-state capacitive touch switch designed to replace traditional mechanical pushbuttons. When a human finger approaches or touches the sensing pad, the change in capacitance triggers the onboard Tontek TTP223-BA6 IC, driving the digital output line.

ParameterSpecification
Operating Voltage2.0V to 5.5V DC (3.3V native for Pico)
Standby Current< 3.0 µA (Low Power Mode at 3V)
Response Time60 ms max in low power mode (220 ms max standby)
Output Drive Current~8 mA sink / ~4 mA source at VDD = 3V
Sensing MediumCan detect touch through plastic, acrylic, wood, glass (up to 3–5 mm thickness)
Onboard IndicatorSurface mount red LED illuminates upon touch
2. Solder Jumper Configuration Matrix (A & B Pads)

On the back of the red PCB are two pairs of solder pads labeled A and B. Bridging these pads with solder changes the output logic and behavior:

4. Enclosure & Hidden Sensor Mounting Notes
Invisible Touch Surfaces: Affix the front touch pad flush against the interior wall of your 3D print or acrylic enclosure using thin double-sided tape. Do not use metal enclosures. Sensitivity remains responsive through acrylic or PLA up to 3.0 mm – 4.0 mm thickness.
5. Pin Wiring to Raspberry Pi Pico
  • VCC → Pico 3V3 (Pin 36)
  • GND → Pico GND (Pin 38)
  • I/O → Any digital GPIO (e.g., GP14 or GP15)
6. CircuitPython Reading Code
import time, board, digitalio

touch = digitalio.DigitalInOut(board.GP14)
touch.direction = digitalio.Direction.INPUT
# Default mode outputs HIGH when touched:
while True:
    if touch.value:
        print("TOUCH DETECTED!")
    time.sleep(0.05)
